# EMBERKEEPER

[![best-game-01](https://make.fun/badge/best-game-01.svg)](https://make.fun/c/best-game-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/best-game-01/b19bay/best-game-01-v2b/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/c/best-game-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/best-game-01/b19bay/best-game-01-v2b/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/c/best-game-01)

You are the Emberkeeper of the last lamp on a storm coast. Each night, wisps rise from the dark sea and drift toward your lamp and toward the ships trying to reach your harbour. Your only weapon is the lamp itself: aim its great beam with the mouse and burn the wisps — nothing burns outside the beam. Hold to focus the beam (narrower, hotter, twice the damage), escort three ships home every night, ring the bell to stun the swarm, and survive all five nights against three named bosses. Oil is life: the beam drinks it, burns earn it back.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://127.0.0.1:8080/
```

Hand the entry in:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Controls: MOUSE aims the beam · HOLD LMB / SPACE focus · E bell · P pause · M mute · R restart.
